Omni-Rewriter · H3 PE

Prompt expansion tuned for MiniMax-H3

Omni-Rewriter turns everyday video intent into typed H3 prompts: shot timing, dialogue tags, camera grammar, and continuity checks with bounded repair. Expand is not generate. The same-seed RAW vs PE compares below is our public PE instance for H3 — denser curation continues.

Open source on GitHub Read the PE harness In-repo showcase
Why PE for H3

H3 rewards prompts that already know the cut

Short user intents rarely specify shot timing, speaker markup, camera motion, or ambient layers. Omni-Rewriter expands transport-neutral intent into a typed H3 dialect, validates it, and repairs failures in a bounded loop. Pairs share seed, aspect, and duration so differences come from the prompt path.

Click a stage to inspect what happens there. Expand stays separate from generate: adapters are optional and evidence-scoped. The VLM pairwise scorer is post-hoc only — scores do not revise prompts.

Stage · Input

RewriteRequest

Transport-neutral intent enters the harness with duration, task route, and metadata such as aspect ratio and seed.

  • Video tasks require duration_seconds
  • Task routes: t2va / i2va / fl2va / …

01

Fencing duel multi-angle

Wide → crash-zoom clash → tip-hit CU → reverse-angle Touché with synced blade audio.

Prompts

RAW

A fencing bout in a bright salle: open wide on two fencers, crash-zoom into a blade clash, hard-cut to a tip-hit extreme close-up, then reverse-angle as the referee raises a hand and calls 'Touché!' in English with lip-sync; synchronize foot scrapes, blade rings, electronic scoring beep, and crowd hush to each cut.

H3 PE

integrated_multimodal_description: Live-action, cinematic, 16:9 aspect ratio. [Shot 1] A wide static shot establishes a bright, sunlit fencing salle with two fencers in white gear facing each other in the en garde position, their blades held high. The fencers advance with quick, rhythmic footwork, creating a tense standoff. [Shot 2] At 00:04.500, a rapid crash-zoom accelerates into the center of the frame as the two blades collide with a sharp metallic ring, the camera shaking slightly from the impact force. [Shot 3] At 00:07.000, a hard cut to an extreme close-up of the fencer's tip piercing the opponent's lamé jacket, the electronic scoring light flashing red instantly. [Shot 4] At 00:09.500, a reverse-angle medium shot shows the referee stepping forward, raising a hand decisively while the fencers freeze. The referee (S1) says: <d>[English] Touché!</d> with precise lip-sync. The scene holds on the referee's authoritative gesture and the fencers' reaction for the remainder of the clip.

overall_soundscape: The soundscape begins with the rhythmic squeak of fencing shoes on the wooden floor and the low hum of the salle. As the blades clash in Shot 2, a sharp, high-pitched metallic ring dominates. In Shot 3, a distinct, loud electronic scoring beep triggers immediately upon the hit. Throughout the sequence, a subtle crowd hush builds, dropping to near silence as the referee speaks in Shot 4, emphasizing the spoken dialogue.

non_diegetic_music: A tense, staccato orchestral string motif that accelerates in tempo during the advance, cuts abruptly to silence at the moment of the blade clash, and remains silent until a single, resolving major chord strikes exactly when the referee says 'Touché!'.

A focused Japanese chef in a white apron stands behind the counter, looking directly at a young tourist. The chef calls out orders with animated gestures. The tourist, holding a menu, looks up and responds. The chef (S1) says: <d>[Japanese] Shio ramen, gochisou sama!</d> The tourist (S2) replies: <d>[English] Yes, please! One miso ramen with extra chashu.</d> The chef nods and turns to the kitchen, while the tourist smiles and waits. The camera slowly pushes in on the steam and the chef's hands working the noodles. [Shot 2] At 00:05.000, the chef vigorously tosses noodles in a wok over a high flame, sending sparks flying as the tourist watches in awe. [Shot 3] At 00:10.000, the finished bowl is placed on the counter with a steaming broth, garnished with green onions and a slice of chashu, as the tourist reaches for chopsticks.

overall_soundscape: Loud kitchen ambience including boiling water, clattering bowls, chopping sounds, and the hiss of a gas stove. The chef's Japanese dialogue and the tourist's English dialogue are clear, distinct, and perfectly lip-synced, cutting through the background noise without distortion.
