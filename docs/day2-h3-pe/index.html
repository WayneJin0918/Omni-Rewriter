The day after on-device acceleration narratives, Omni-Rewriter focuses on what the model actually consumes: structured H3 timelines, dialogue tags, camera grammar, and continuity — with deterministic validation and bounded repair. Expand is not generate.

01 — Why PE for H3

H3 rewards prompts that already know the cut

MiniMax-H3 is an omni-modal video generator with native audio. Short user intents rarely specify shot timing, speaker markup, camera motion, or ambient layers. Omni-Rewriter turns transport-neutral intent into a typed H3 dialect, checks it, repairs failures in a bounded loop, then renders text the runtime can consume. Same seed comparisons below keep generation settings fixed so differences come from the prompt path.

02 — Pipeline

Analyze · draft · validate · repair · render

A PE profile does not prove generation-runtime compatibility. Adapters and live runners are optional, version-scoped, and evidence-backed. Untested paths stay labeled unverified.

01RewriteRequestPrompt, duration, task, metadata.
02Analyze & draftPlan invariants, state, transitions, evidence.
03ValidateDeterministic H3 grammar and timeline checks.
04Bounded repairRetry only with errors and required invariants.
05Dialect renderH3 text out; optional generate adapter next.

Case 01 multilingual_dialogue

Tokyo ramen bilingual dialogue

RAW prompt

In a Tokyo ramen shop, the chef calls orders in Japanese and a tourist replies in English; both languages must be audible and lip-synced over kitchen sounds.
Expanded H3 PE same seed · validated dialect 
integrated_multimodal_description: Live-action, cinematic, 16:9 aspect ratio. [Shot 1] Inside a bustling Tokyo ramen shop with steam rising from large pots and wooden counters. A focused Japanese chef in a white apron stands behind the counter, looking directly at a young tourist. The chef calls out orders with animated gestures. The tourist, holding a menu, looks up and responds. The chef (S1) says: <d>[Japanese] Shio ramen, gochisou sama!</d> The tourist (S2) replies: <d>[English] Yes, please! One miso ramen with extra chashu.</d> The chef nods and turns to the kitchen, while the tourist smiles and waits. The camera slowly pushes in on the steam and the chef's hands working the noodles. [Shot 2] At 00:05.000, the chef vigorously tosses noodles in a wok over a high flame, sending sparks flying as the tourist watches in awe. [Shot 3] At 00:10.000, the finished bowl is placed on the counter with a steaming broth, garnished with green onions and a slice of chashu, as the tourist reaches for chopsticks.

overall_soundscape: Loud kitchen ambience including boiling water, clattering bowls, chopping sounds, and the hiss of a gas stove. The chef's Japanese dialogue and the tourist's English dialogue are clear, distinct, and perfectly lip-synced, cutting through the background noise without distortion.

non_diegetic_music: None

As the noodles arc, a massive burst of roaring orange flame erupts from the wok, illuminating the chef's determined face. The sounds are perfectly synchronized with the visual motions of the chef's movements.
