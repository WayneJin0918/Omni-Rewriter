# Write H3 Ref2VA Prompts

Convert a complete target-video timeline plus reusable media conditions into the exact H3 Ref2VA contract. Read `references/ref2va-contract.md` before serialization. For sequential stories or dense action, also read `references/long-form-action.md`.

## Base-First Principle

Write the complete intended video before wrapping it in reference analysis. The Base timeline must independently specify story, timestamped shots, scene geometry, subjects, performance, action, camera, lighting, dialogue, effects, soundscape, music, and persistent end state.

References add provenance and retention constraints. They do not replace missing target-video content.

## Workflow

1. Inventory media conditions in exact request order and map the resulting `<Picture N>`, `<Video N>`, and `<Audio N>` labels.
2. Assign stable `<Subject N>` identities or reusable attributes. Never reuse a subject ID for another entity.
3. Separate invariants from degrees of freedom. State exactly which identity, attribute, state, composition, motion, style, or timbre each reference controls.
4. Build one causal target timeline with enough duration for onset, development, completion, and persistence.
5. Choose shots and camera movement only to expose required evidence. Preserve screen direction and carried motion across cuts.
6. For direct continuation, begin from the final moving state of the supplied tail video and restate the scene and current action explicitly.
7. Add the reference wrapper without shortening `detailed_description`.
8. Validate with `python3 scripts/validate_h3_prompt.py PROMPT --mode ref2va --duration SECONDS`.
9. Inspect the rendered output before accepting identity, action, dialogue, or endpoint compliance.

## Reference Discipline

- Use the minimum clean reference set needed by the shot.
- After a new recurring subject first appears, extract a clean frontal or three-quarter identity frame from an accepted output, record its source timestamp, and use it from the following shot onward.
- Prefer a 2-3 second tail video for direct motion continuity. Do not pad it to target duration.
- Strip a continuation video's soundtrack when only visual continuity is wanted. In the current self-hosted runtime, a video with audio also contributes an audio condition.
- Use separate clean per-speaker audio for voice timbre. Mixed music, effects, and multiple voices are weak speaker references.
- Keep seed and all non-prompt settings fixed when comparing caption variants.

## Action And Ending

For action, describe preparation, trajectory, contact or deliberate miss, force response, and recovery. Use explicit time windows and diversify body mechanics instead of repeating straight punches. Allocate the final 1.5-3 seconds to persistent evidence of the outcome.

Do not infer success from the request. Review the full video and a temporal contact sheet sampled more frequently than the shortest important action beat.

## Output

Output only the six-section model-ready Ref2VA prompt. Do not emit JSON, markdown commentary, negative prompts, generation parameters, or LTX memory-slot syntax.
